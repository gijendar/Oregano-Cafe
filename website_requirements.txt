22. PRICE CALCULATION
============================================================

Item total:

ITEM PRICE × QUANTITY

Grand Total:

SUM OF ALL ITEM TOTALS

Example:

₹290 × 2 = ₹580
₹180 × 1 = ₹180
₹30 × 2 = ₹60

TOTAL = ₹820

Do not add hidden charges.

Do not invent taxes or fees.

The supplied menu states that the cafe does not add
extra taxes or hidden charges to the bill.

50. PROFIT / LOSS CALCULATION
============================================================

Formula:

TOTAL EARNINGS =
SUM OF COMPLETED ORDER TOTALS

TOTAL EXPENSES =
SUM OF EXPENSES

NET PROFIT =
TOTAL EARNINGS - TOTAL EXPENSES


Example:

Earnings:
₹10,000

Expenses:
₹3,500

Profit:
+₹6,500


If:

Earnings:
₹5,000

Expenses:
₹6,500

Net:

-₹1,500

Display:

NET LOSS

-₹1,500
